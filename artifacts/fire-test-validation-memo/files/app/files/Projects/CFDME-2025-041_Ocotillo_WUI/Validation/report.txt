1. TEST STANDARD AND METHOD
The evaluation reported here was performed in accordance with ASTM E119, Standard Test Methods for Fire Tests
of Building Construction and Materials (current edition). One vertical fire-endurance test was performed on the wall
assembly described in Section 2, using the standard time-temperature curve prescribed by ASTM E119. The
exposed face of the assembly was subjected to the E119 standard curve delivered by the laboratory's natural-gas
vertical wall furnace, with furnace temperatures monitored by nine furnace thermocouples symmetrically distributed
on the exposed face per the standard.
The assembly was tested as a non-load-bearing partition. The applicable fire-endurance criteria for a
non-load-bearing wall under ASTM E119 are the following two thermal-transmission conditions on the unexposed
face:
• Average unexposed-face criterion. The average temperature rise measured by nine thermocouples
   symmetrically disposed on the unexposed face of the specimen shall not exceed 139 degC (250 degF) above the
   initial ambient temperature at any point in the test.
• Single-point criterion. The temperature rise at any single thermocouple on the unexposed face shall not exceed
   181 degC (325 degF) above the initial ambient temperature at any point in the test.
Per ASTM E119, the earlier of the two thermal-transmission criteria governs the reported fire endurance for the
specimen.


2. SPECIMEN DESCRIPTION
One 3.05 m × 3.05 m (10 ft × 10 ft) specimen was constructed by the client at the Red Butte laboratory prior to test.
The specimen is a double-wythe 3D-printed concrete wall assembly consisting of two printed concrete leaves
separated by an open-air cavity and connected by continuous truss-type steel ties.

2.1 Concrete leaves
• Number of leaves: two, oriented parallel and in registration.
• Leaf thickness: each leaf 50 mm as printed and cured (siliceous aggregate mix).
• Aggregate: siliceous.
• Placement: wet-on-wet extruded 3D printing; nominal 20 mm bead pitch; leaves printed vertically on the
   specimen frame and cured to the manufacturer's 28-day protocol prior to shipment for test.

2.2 Cavity
• Cavity width: 205 mm between the interior surfaces of the two leaves.
• Cavity contents: open air. No insulation, no fill, no membrane, no fire-stop material was present in the cavity as
   constructed for this test.

2.3 Overall specimen thickness
• Overall: 305 mm (50 + 205 + 50).
2.4 Wall ties
Continuous truss-type (triangular zigzag) ties fabricated from 9-gauge (3.8 mm) galvanized steel wire connect the
two leaves. Tie schedule:

3. INSTRUMENTATION
Instrumentation on the unexposed face was applied by Red Butte technicians per ASTM E119. All thermocouples
were Type K, 20 AWG, glass-braid insulated, fixed to the unexposed face of the specimen with pads per the
standard.

3.1 Unexposed-face thermocouple layout
Nine thermocouples, designated TC-1 through TC-9, were installed on the unexposed face in the symmetric pattern
prescribed by ASTM E119: one thermocouple at the geometric center of the specimen, four at the midpoints of
imaginary quadrants, and four additional thermocouples such that no thermocouple lies within 305 mm (12 in) of a
specimen edge and thermocouples are approximately equidistant from one another. The average of the readings
from TC-1 through TC-9 is the value used for the average unexposed-face criterion.
A tenth thermocouple, TC-10, was installed as a single-point plotter and continuous strip-chart reference; TC-10
readings are recorded but are not part of the nine-thermocouple average.

3.2 Position of TC-6 relative to tie course
The specimen was surveyed for tie positions before instrumentation was applied. During layout of the nine
unexposed-face thermocouples, thermocouple 6 fell over one of the horizontal courses of steel wall ties: TC-6 is
located directly opposite a 3.8 mm truss-type tie embedded in the outer (unexposed) leaf. This positioning is retained
as the E119 symmetric layout was applied without adjustment; the location is documented in the plan diagram below.

The tie course crossing behind TC-6 constitutes a metallic path from the exposed leaf to the unexposed leaf at that
location. Readings from TC-6 are therefore expected to reflect the local thermal signature of that path and are
reported alongside the eight other unexposed-face thermocouples without adjustment or omission.

3.4 Furnace and ambient instrumentation
• Nine furnace thermocouples (TCF-1 through TCF-9) per E119, symmetrically placed within 6 in of the exposed
   face, monitored delivery of the standard time-temperature curve.
• Two ambient thermocouples (TC-A1, TC-A2) recorded laboratory ambient at the unexposed side, from which the
   initial ambient reference used for the rise criteria was derived.
• Initial ambient (average of TC-A1, TC-A2 at t = 0): 21.0 degC.
3.5 Data acquisition
All thermocouple channels were logged at 1 Hz to a computerized data acquisition system with NIST-traceable
channel calibration. TC-10 was additionally recorded on an independent single-point strip-chart plotter to provide a
mechanical secondary trace.


4. TEST CONDUCT
The instrumented specimen was mounted in the laboratory's vertical wall furnace on the morning of 09/24/2025. The
exposed face was oriented toward the furnace chamber. The specimen was clamped at the perimeter with the same
frame and gasket detail routinely used for non-load-bearing partition specimens at this laboratory.
The furnace was ignited and the exposed face was subjected to the ASTM E119 standard time-temperature curve
from t = 0 onward. Furnace temperatures tracked the standard curve within the tolerance band prescribed by ASTM
E119 for the full duration of the test (deviation area within the specified 10% at 1 hr and 7.5% at 2 hr and beyond).
The specimen was monitored continuously. The test was conducted to termination on the governing
thermal-transmission criterion as defined in Section 1 above; no hose-stream retest was performed as none was
contracted. No load was applied to the specimen (non-load-bearing configuration).
Test start: 09:02 MDT, 09/24/2025. Test termination: 11:00 MDT, 09/24/2025 (118 min after start), at which point the
governing thermal-transmission criterion was reached. Data acquisition continued for 45 additional minutes past
termination to characterize the full unexposed-face response, at which point the average-rise criterion was also
reached (see Section 5).


5. RESULTS
5.1 Average unexposed-face temperature rise (nine-thermocouple mean)
The arithmetic mean of TC-1 through TC-9, expressed as rise above the initial ambient of 21.0 degC, is the value
evaluated against the average criterion.
• Criterion: 139 degC (250 degF) rise above initial ambient.
• The nine-thermocouple mean rise reached 139 degC at 163 min after test start.
5.2 Single-point unexposed-face temperature rise
The maximum rise across the nine unexposed-face thermocouples, at each instant, was tracked continuously against
the single-point criterion.
• Criterion: 181 degC (325 degF) rise above initial ambient.
• The maximum rise reached 181 degC at 118 min after test start.
• Governing thermocouple at the moment the criterion was reached: thermocouple 6, positioned over a horizontal
   steel tie course as described in Section 3.2 and shown in the plan diagram.
At 118 min, TC-6 recorded 181 degC rise above ambient while the nine-thermocouple mean rise stood at
approximately 96 degC. The remaining eight unexposed-face thermocouples (TC-1 through TC-5 and TC-7 through
TC-9) had not individually reached the single-point limit at that time. TC-10 (single-point plotter, near geometric
center) tracked closely with the mean of TC-1 through TC-9 throughout.

5.3 Governing endurance
Per ASTM E119, the earlier of the two thermal-transmission criteria governs the reported fire endurance:

 Criterion                                Value                                    Time reached
 Average unexposed-face rise              139 degC                                 163 min
 (nine-TC mean)
 Single-point rise (any single TC)        181 degC                                 118 min (at thermocouple 6)

The single-point criterion was reached first at 118 min and therefore governs the reported fire endurance for this
specimen.
> Reported endurance D = 118 min.


6. CONCLUSION
Under the conditions of ASTM E119 (standard time-temperature curve, non-load-bearing, no applied load, no
hose-stream retest contracted), the double-wythe 3D-printed concrete wall assembly described in Section 2 exhibited
a measured fire endurance of 118 min, governed by the single-point criterion at thermocouple 6, which was
positioned over a horizontal truss-type steel tie course. The average unexposed-face criterion was reached later at
163 min and does not govern.
This report documents the measured thermal-transmission behavior of the specimen tested. Certification of a
fire-resistance rating for the assembly is the domain of the certifying fire-protection engineer of record for the project
and is not within the scope of this laboratory or this report.
